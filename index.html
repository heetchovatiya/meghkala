About the studio

Every stitch, considered.

Meghkala began as a quiet hobby — a hook, a ball of yarn, and slow evenings — and grew into a small studio for people who love things made unhurried. No factories, no shortcuts. Just careful hands turning yarn into pieces meant to be kept.

Each design is worked in small batches, so what you receive is genuinely one of a kind. Colours, textures and little details vary gently from piece to piece — that's the handmade promise.
